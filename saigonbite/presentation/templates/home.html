How ordering works

  1. Pick a hub near you. A busy hub is marked, and a quiet one takes 5,000 VND off the delivery fee.
  2. Choose your dishes and the address. Add one voucher if you have one.
  3. Check the full price, then pay cash on delivery or online. Pressing twice still places one order.
  4. Follow your order under My orders until it arrives.
