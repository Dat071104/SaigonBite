\textbf{Abstract} \\ \hline
SaigonBite connects customer ordering, kitchen decisions, delivery and operational reporting. This report records the requirements, design, implementation and evidence for the Part 2 demonstration. The online payment option is simulated and refund obligations are recorded without a payout. Recorded test and deployment results are scoped to the documented runs. \\ \hline
