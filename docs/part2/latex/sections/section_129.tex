\textbf{Mapping rules.}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One class, one table; \texttt{id} becomes the primary key.} Attributes keep their names and become columns. Money is whole VND (INTEGER), Boolean is INTEGER 0/1 with a CHECK, timestamps are UTC ISO text, and voucher dates are Vietnam calendar dates.

\item \textbf{Associations become foreign keys} on the "many" side (for example \texttt{orders.\allowbreak{}customer\_\allowbreak{}id}). A one-to-zero-or-one association gets a UNIQUE foreign key (\texttt{payment.\allowbreak{}order\_\allowbreak{}id}, \texttt{delivery.\allowbreak{}order\_\allowbreak{}id}, \texttt{customer.\allowbreak{}user\_\allowbreak{}id}).

\item \textbf{Composition} of OrderLine in Order (the whole owns its parts) is the NOT NULL foreign key \texttt{order\_\allowbreak{}line.\allowbreak{}order\_\allowbreak{}id}; lines are never deleted on their own.

\item \textbf{Association class} HubMenuItem becomes the table \texttt{hub\_\allowbreak{}menu\_\allowbreak{}item} with the composite primary key (\texttt{hub\_\allowbreak{}id}, \texttt{menu\_\allowbreak{}item\_\allowbreak{}id}) and the attribute \texttt{is\_\allowbreak{}available} of the pair.

\item \textbf{Generalization} LocalCustomer / TouristCustomer uses single-table inheritance: one \texttt{customer} table with the discriminator \texttt{customer\_\allowbreak{}type} and a CHECK that requires a phone for LOCAL and passport, email and nationality for TOURIST.

\item \textbf{State attributes} become TEXT columns whose CHECK lists exactly the states of the state machines in 2.2 (for example \texttt{orders.\allowbreak{}state}).

\item \textbf{Derived attributes} are not stored when they can be computed (hub load, busy/quiet); the order totals S, D, F, Q and T are stored as the agreed snapshot and tied together by \texttt{CHECK (total\_\allowbreak{}amount = item\_\allowbreak{}subtotal \ensuremath{-} voucher\_\allowbreak{}discount + delivery\_\allowbreak{}fee \ensuremath{-} quiet\_\allowbreak{}discount)}.

\item \textbf{Archive instead of delete:} \texttt{archived\_\allowbreak{}at} on Hub, Brand, MenuItem, Address, Voucher and Order; \texttt{is\_\allowbreak{}active} on UserAccount. The only physical delete is unlisting an item at a hub (\texttt{MenuDAO} removes the \texttt{hub\_\allowbreak{}menu\_\allowbreak{}item} row); earlier orders are unaffected because order lines keep their own name and price snapshot.

\item \textbf{Rules the CHECKs cannot express} are enforced by triggers: \texttt{customer\_\allowbreak{}role\_\allowbreak{}insert} and \texttt{customer\_\allowbreak{}role\_\allowbreak{}update} allow a customer profile only on a CUSTOMER account, and \texttt{linked\_\allowbreak{}customer\_\allowbreak{}role} stops such an account from changing role. A partial unique index \texttt{idx\_\allowbreak{}offer\_\allowbreak{}one\_\allowbreak{}open} allows at most one OFFERED replacement per order.

\end{itemize}
