Use case name & UC-05 --- Decide an incoming order: confirm, reject, or offer a replacement the customer agrees to. Author: 722I0004 \ensuremath{\cdot} AI-assisted (Codex) \\
Scenario / trigger & Kitchen staff opens an incoming own-hub PLACED order and chooses confirm, reject or a whole-line replacement for a dish currently unavailable. The customer answers separately through SUC-03; the kitchen request never waits inside a transaction for the answer. \\
Actors & Primary: Kitchen staff. Customer participates in the separate SUC-03 response session (companion fragment with Customer + :System). Every request rechecks active role and owner/hub authorization. \\
Related use cases & Figure 2.1B shows Kitchen staff and Customer participation, with the Customer response served by SUC-03 in Figure 2.1A. UC-01 supplies the original order. SUC-05 starts preparing/marks ready after confirmation; SUC-06 changes own-hub availability. These are serving/sequence links, not invented \guillemotleft{}include\guillemotright{} relations. SUC-04 extends SUC-03 at order action selected when cancellation is requested from PLACED/CONFIRMED. \\
Stakeholders and interests & Kitchen: only own-hub decisions and one open offer. Customer: unchanged quantity, equal/lower offered unit price, consent to replacement amounts and preserved captured payment. Operator: atomic line/totals/offer/payment updates and explicit refund owed without a payout claim. \\
Pre-conditions & An active Kitchen staff session is assigned to a hub. A candidate incoming order exists; its own-hub PLACED state, open-offer status and current source/target eligibility are guards rechecked at each command. Customer response requires a separate active Customer session and own order. \\
Post-conditions & \textbf{UC05-PC01} confirm: own-hub PLACED \ensuremath{\rightarrow} CONFIRMED\newline{}\textbf{UC05-PC02} reject: REJECTED + reason + BR-17 + offer WITHDRAWN\newline{}\textbf{UC05-PC03} offer: one OFFERED offer per \S{}3.3\newline{}\textbf{UC05-PC04} accepted offer applied per \S{}3.3 5--7\newline{}\textbf{UC05-MG01} wrong hub / open offer / stale / invalid target \ensuremath{\rightarrow} order unchanged\newline{}PC01--PC04 are alternative outcomes at their respective operation boundaries. MG01 preserves Order; invalid-target acceptance may still withdraw the exact offer and return a reason. Later rejection/cancellation is never reversed. \\
Flow of activities & Numbered Actor / System main flow and A01--A05 / E01--E04 below form this field. Main steps 1--5 confirm; A02 rejects; A03 creates an offer; response C1--C6 is an explicitly separate SUC-03 session. \\
Exception conditions & Wrong hub/role/state, open offer on confirm or another offer, invalid source/target, stale answer and write failure produce controlled responses. Every failure ends with UC05-MG01 (Order unchanged); invalid-target acceptance alone changes the exact offer to WITHDRAWN and shows its reason, without adding a persisted reason field. \\

A01 & Step 2/5, staff refreshes board/details & Calls \texttt{viewKitchenOrders()} / \texttt{viewKitchenOrder(orderId)} again; reads current facts without mutation. & Rejoin 2 or end read session; queries never change Order (UC05-MG01 invariant). \\
A02 & Step 3, chooses reject & Staff submits \texttt{rejectOrder(orderId, reason)}. System requires own-hub PLACED and nonempty reason; atomically sets REJECTED, reason and closed\_at, withdraws any open offer and applies BR-17: COD\ensuremath{\rightarrow}VOIDED; ONLINE\ensuremath{\rightarrow}REFUND\_PENDING with refund\_amount=C, replacing any prior partial amount. Returns \texttt{rejectedOrder}. & End kitchen decision: UC05-PC02. Wrong/stale/invalid reason\ensuremath{\rightarrow}E01; failed save\ensuremath{\rightarrow}E04. \\
A03 & Step 3, chooses replacement & Staff submits \texttt{offerReplacement(orderId, orderLineId, targetItemId)}. One transaction rechecks own-hub PLACED, no OFFERED offer and selected line belongs to this Order. Source dish must be \textbf{currently unavailable at this hub} (sold out, unlisted or archived). Target must be ACTIVE with ACTIVE brand, listed and AVAILABLE at this same hub, different from source, absent from other order lines, and unit price \ensuremath{\leq} source snapshot price; cross-brand is allowed. Inserts exactly one OFFERED offer with original/new name and price snapshots, line link and offered\_at; quantity unchanged. Returns \texttt{offeredReplacement}. Order remains PLACED and still counts toward load. & End kitchen request: UC05-PC03. Continue later in independent C1--C6. Guards fail\ensuremath{\rightarrow}E01/E02. \\
A04 & After C5 accepted response & Staff refreshes current board (A01); if another dish is unavailable, another sequential offer may be made while PLACED because resolved offers remain history. If no open offer remains, staff can confirm at step 3. & Rejoin 2/3 in a new request; never automatically confirm on Customer acceptance. \\
A05 & During offer wait or after acceptance & Customer may cancel via SUC-04 from own PLACED/CONFIRMED; open offer\ensuremath{\rightarrow}WITHDRAWN, Order\ensuremath{\rightarrow}CANCELLED, BR-17 full C (never C+partial). Kitchen may reject PLACED under A02. A concurrent response rechecks serialized current facts. & End that supported action. Later answer uses E03 and cannot revive CANCELLED/REJECTED; UC05-MG01 for refused answer. \\

Ownership failure discloses no foreign order details. Never revives a terminal order. & End response: UC05-MG01; customer may read again at C1. \\

Fixed contract example (not a test result): S=100,000, D=10,000, F=15,000, Q=5,000 gives T=C=100,000. Accepted replacement S\_new=90,000 gives D\_new=10,000 and T\_new=90,000. COD payable=90,000; ONLINE keeps C=100,000 with 10,000 owed. Later cancel owes full 100,000. A delivered order retains T\_new=90,000 for operational sales; the owed 10,000 is separate, never subtracted again.
