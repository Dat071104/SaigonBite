Dat plans to record the demonstration on the deployed site. The video link is \LinkVideo. The screenshots below come from the earlier P6 deployment evidence on 27 October 2026 and illustrate the steps; a new recording may produce different order IDs or amounts. Use the demo-only accounts in the project README: \texttt{customer}, \texttt{kitchen} and \texttt{shipper}, with the shared demo password shown there. Use a new order and verify the current on-screen amount at each step.

\begin{itemize}
\item Sign in as the customer, choose an available hub and dish, review the quote and confirm a cash-on-delivery order. Note its order ID and amount.

\end{itemize}
