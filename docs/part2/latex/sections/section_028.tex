\textbf{2.2.2 i.}\par
\textbf{UC-02 --- Deliver an order: pick up, deliver with cash collection, or report failure (author: 722I0004)}

Author: 722I0004 \ensuremath{\cdot} AI-assisted (Codex)

\textbf{Use case name}

UC-02 --- Deliver an order: pick up, deliver with cash collection, or report failure.

\textbf{Scenario / trigger}

A shipper requests ready deliveries, claims an unassigned READY order, and later records either successful handover or a failed delivery. Pickup and finish are separate requests; leaving the session does not undo a successful pickup. Main success below uses COD; ONLINE and failure are explicit alternatives.

\textbf{Actors}

Primary: Shipper, an authenticated ACTIVE UserAccount with role SHIPPER. The customer receives the food in the business process but does not send a system message in this use case. No payment provider participates: ONLINE was captured by the simulator during UC-01.

\textbf{Related use cases}

Figure 2.1B associates Shipper with UC-02. It defines no include/extend relation for UC-02. SUC-05 supplies READY orders and their AWAITING\_PICKUP Delivery; UC-01 supplies Order/Payment and immutable delivery-address snapshots; SUC-01 supplies authentication; SUC-08 maintains active accounts; UC-04 reads the outcome. These are serving references, not invented UML include/extend relations.

\textbf{Stakeholders and interests}

Shipper needs a fair atomic claim, minimum necessary contact details and exact cash due. Customer needs correct delivery and no duplicate collection. Kitchen needs an unambiguous handover. Management needs paired Order/Delivery states, times, cash and refund obligations for reports. Admin must not deactivate a shipper holding a PICKED\_UP delivery (BR-25).

\textbf{Pre-conditions}

Every request rechecks the authenticated active SHIPPER context (BR-25); identity comes from the session. Pickup requires Order READY and its single unassigned Delivery AWAITING\_PICKUP. Any active shipper can claim any READY order, including while holding other deliveries; there is no one-active-delivery cap. Finish requires the same Order OUT\_FOR\_DELIVERY, Delivery PICKED\_UP and the authenticated shipper assigned to it. Order retains S/D/F/Q/T, address snapshot and Payment; COD is PENDING and due T; ONLINE is already PAID with captured amount C. A prior accepted replacement may leave a partial refund owed while ONLINE remains PAID.

Each accepted command saves related state/time/payment facts atomically. Successful ONLINE delivery keeps PAID, C and any partial refund owed; it takes no cash. Failed delivery saves a nonempty failureReason and finished/closed timestamps; COD becomes VOIDED; ONLINE becomes REFUND\_PENDING with refund\_amount=C, replacing any partial obligation, in the same change (BR-17). Refund payout is deferred, never claimed completed. A refused command changes no rows, preserving any previously committed pickup or finish. Read requests write nothing. BR-12 exposes only delivery contact and the stored address snapshot; passport is excluded from shipper views and logs.
