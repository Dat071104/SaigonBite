\textbf{Archive and persistence notes}

An archived parent hides itself and its children from operational lists (boards, menus and checkout). Authorised history, receipts, reports and refund obligations still include them. Archive is an operational visibility rule, not physical child deletion or permission to erase snapshots. SUC-09 accepts only DELIVERED, DELIVERY\_FAILED, REJECTED or CANCELLED orders. Customer deactivation is through the linked UserAccount; it does not erase the profile. HubMenuItem unlisting removes only that association row, and historical OrderLine snapshots survive. Seed rows are test setup, never a ``Create''.

Not persisted: Quote, ReportFilter, report rows and receipt (computed views). No Shipper class/table: SHIPPER is a UserAccount role linked from Delivery.
