Both ends of each ordinary association have multiplicities. Each Customer has one UserAccount; an account has zero or one Customer profile (one when its role is CUSTOMER). A Customer has zero or more Addresses; every Address has one owner. A kitchen account has one Hub, while other account roles have none. Brand owns the catalogue affiliation of zero or more MenuItems. Hub and MenuItem have a many-to-many \textbf{listed at} association, with \textbf{HubMenuItem as its association class} and availability belonging to that pair.

An Order belongs to one Customer, Hub and selected Address; an Address may subsequently be archived without changing the stored delivery-address snapshot. An Order optionally uses one Voucher. Order \textbf{composes} one or more OrderLines; each line belongs to exactly one Order and refers to one MenuItem while retaining its own name/price/quantity snapshots. Each Order has exactly one Payment, zero or one Delivery, and zero or more historical ReplacementOffers. Each offer points to exactly one line of that same Order and one replacement-target MenuItem. A line or target item can appear in many historical offers; at most one offer is OFFERED for an Order at a time.

The Delivery assignment refers to zero or one UserAccount of role SHIPPER; a shipper account can have many deliveries, including concurrent assignments under BR-25. No Shipper class is added. Quote, report filters/rows and receipts are computed views and are not persisted. Archive visibility of dependent transaction records follows the parent Order; separate archive attributes on every child are unnecessary. Busy/quiet/workload and waiting for customer are derived classifications. Payment \texttt{amount} is captured C for ONLINE; \texttt{refund\_\allowbreak{}amount} records an obligation, including a partial obligation while PAID. REFUNDED/payout remains future scope.
