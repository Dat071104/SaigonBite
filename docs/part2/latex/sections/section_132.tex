The integration walkthrough connects the customer, kitchen and shipper paths through the same order. On 27 October 2026, the local P5 browser run at commit \texttt{252ada1} passed 60 of 60 recorded steps across seven scenarios and a CSRF check. In its main path, customer order 11 was visible at its own kitchen; access from another hub was refused. The kitchen and customer then exercised a replacement, and the delivery path continued through pickup and delivery. Separate browser profiles represented the roles. Screenshots and the checks for each step are in \texttt{docs/part2/test\_report/integration\_2026-10-27/README.md} and the S3--S5 folders. This result is a browser demonstration, not proof that all possible business cases were exercised.

The deployed walkthrough on 27 October 2026 adapted scenarios S3--S5 to existing order 9 on the VM. The evidence records 22 of 22 steps passing, including cross-hub access checks, replacement and acceptance, kitchen transitions, a pickup race, incorrect cash and paid delivery. The original unrelated order 10 and a separate seed order 7 were checked later in supplementary browser checks; they were not asserted at the same time as the earlier isolation and pickup tests. The deployed run is therefore an adapted rerun, not a replay of the exact local sequence. Evidence: \texttt{docs/part2/test\_report/deploy\_2026-10-27/README.md} and \texttt{scenarios/results.json}, deployment tag \texttt{p6-deploy-20261027} at \texttt{28d6b33}.
